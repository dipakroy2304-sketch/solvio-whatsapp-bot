SOLVIO Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

1. Introduction

SOLVIO is a WhatsApp-based personal follow-up assistant designed to help users keep track of promises, pending payments, refunds, deliveries and other important commitments.

We respect your privacy and aim to handle your information responsibly. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be processed when you interact with SOLVIO.

2. Information We May Collect

When you communicate with SOLVIO through WhatsApp, we may process:

  • Your WhatsApp phone number.
  • The messages you send to SOLVIO.
  • Names or descriptions of people mentioned in your messages.
  • Amounts, dates and details of pending commitments.
  • Information you provide when requesting a follow-up or reminder.

3. How We Use Your Information

Information may be used to:

  • Receive and understand your WhatsApp messages.
  • Help organise personal follow-ups and commitments.
  • Respond to your requests.
  • Provide reminders and status updates when those features are available.
  • Maintain, troubleshoot and improve the service.

4. Data Storage and Processing

SOLVIO is currently in its prototype stage. Messages and phone numbers may be processed by our application server and may appear in technical logs used for testing and troubleshooting.

A permanent user-record database and full reminder-management functionality are not yet operational. If these features are introduced, this policy will be updated to explain the information stored, how it is used and how users can request its deletion.

5. Third-Party Services

SOLVIO uses WhatsApp and Meta's services to communicate with users. Our application may also use hosting and technical service providers to operate the service.

These providers may process information as necessary to deliver their services and operate the application. SOLVIO does not sell users' personal information.

6. Sharing of Information

We do not intend to sell or rent personal information to third parties. Information may be processed by service providers supporting SOLVIO or disclosed where required by applicable law.

SOLVIO does not automatically contact people mentioned in your messages. Any third-party follow-up communication would require an appropriate user-approved feature and action.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information processed through SOLVIO. However, no internet-based service or electronic transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Please avoid sending passwords, banking credentials or other highly sensitive information through the service.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

Information may remain in technical logs for as long as reasonably necessary for testing, troubleshooting and service operation. Retention periods may depend on the hosting provider.

To request deletion of information associated with your WhatsApp number, email us at dipakroy2304@gmail.com. Please include your WhatsApp phone number and write "SOLVIO Data Deletion Request" in the subject line.

9. Children's Privacy

SOLVIO is not intended for children under the age of 18. We do not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when SOLVIO's features or data practices change. The updated version will be published on this page with a revised date.

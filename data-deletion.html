SOLVIO Data Deletion Instructions

Last updated: 21 November 2026

1. Your Right to Request Deletion

SOLVIO respects your privacy. You may request deletion of personal information associated with your use of SOLVIO, including your WhatsApp phone number and messages processed by our application.

2. How to Request Data Deletion

To request deletion, follow these steps:

  1. Send an email to dipakroy2304@gmail.com.
  2. Use the subject line: SOLVIO Data Deletion Request.
  3. Include your WhatsApp phone number with country code.
  4. Write that you want your SOLVIO-related information deleted.

You do not need to share your WhatsApp password or any account password to make this request.

3. What Happens Next?

We will review your request and may contact you if we need reasonable information to verify that the request relates to your account.

We will take reasonable steps to delete information held by SOLVIO within a reasonable period, subject to applicable legal requirements and any information that must be retained by service providers under their own policies.

4. Current Prototype

SOLVIO is currently a prototype. It does not yet have a permanent user-record database. Some messages and phone numbers may be processed or appear in technical logs for testing and troubleshooting.

Deletion requests will be handled according to the information that SOLVIO and its service providers actually retain.
